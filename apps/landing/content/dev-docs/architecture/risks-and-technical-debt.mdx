The **Risks and Technical Debt** log (arc42 §11) records known architectural trade-offs, security exposures, and structural debt items across Innostes ERP. Each entry is assigned an owner, an impact rating, and a clear remediation strategy.

---

## 1. Risk Assessment Methodology

Risks are assessed using a standard qualitative matrix:
- **Likelihood**: Low (unlikely to occur), Medium (possible in normal operations), High (frequent or trivial to exploit).
- **Impact**: Low (minor inconvenience), Medium (degraded service or technical debt), High (data breach, platform downtime, or compliance violation).
