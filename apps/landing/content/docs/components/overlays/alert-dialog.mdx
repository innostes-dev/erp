## Writing good confirmations

- Title asks the question: "Delete Acme Corp?", not "Are you sure?".
- Description states the consequence: what's lost, and whether it can be undone.
- The confirm button repeats the action ("Delete customer"), not "OK".

## Accessibility

- `role="alertdialog"`, labelled by the title and described by the description.
- Focus starts inside the dialog; Escape cancels.
