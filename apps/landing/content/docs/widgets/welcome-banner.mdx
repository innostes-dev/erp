## When to use

Use `WelcomeBanner` as the welcoming centerpiece at the top of high-level role dashboards:

- **Department hubs**: Greeting procurement managers, warehouse leads, or finance officers.
- **Onboarding dashboards**: Welcoming newly joined staff with links to initial setup tasks.
- **Executive summaries**: Presenting high-level fiscal dates alongside organizational mottos.

**Do not use WelcomeBanner for:**
- Entity record detail pages (like a specific purchase order or invoice) — use [PageHeader](/ui/docs/components/layout/page-header).
- Compact KPI metric displays — use [MetricCard](/ui/docs/widgets/metric-card).
- In-form instructional callouts — use [Alert](/ui/docs/components/feedback/alert).

## Examples

### Gradient variant with action buttons and inspirational quote

The standard administrative dashboard greeting:

```tsx
<WelcomeBanner
  eyebrow="Fiscal Year 2026-27 · Bengaluru Division"
  title="Welcome back, Priya Sharma"
  description="You have 3 purchase orders pending approval and 1 GST filing discrepancy to resolve."
  variant="gradient"
  quote="Integrity in procurement delivers resilience in supply."
  actions={
    <div className="flex flex-wrap gap-2">
      <Button size="sm">Create PO</Button>
      <Button size="sm" variant="outline">Export tax report</Button>
    </div>
  }
/>
```

### Brand and plain variants

- `variant="gradient"` (default): Uses a gentle wash of the primary theme tint.
- `variant="brand"`: Solid primary background with light text for high-contrast executive portals.
- `variant="plain"`: Clean card surface with standard border.

```tsx
<WelcomeBanner
  variant="brand"
  title="Supply Chain Operations"
  description="Northwind Traders Bengaluru Central Depot"
/>
```

## Accessibility

`WelcomeBanner` renders semantic section landmarks.

### Keyboard navigation

<KeyboardTable
  entries={[

  ]}

### ARIA attributes

- The container renders as a `<section data-slot="welcome-banner">`.
- Heading titles render with semantic hierarchy appropriate for top-of-page introductions.
- Decorative illustrations render with `aria-hidden="true"`.

## Guidelines

<DoDont
  do={{
