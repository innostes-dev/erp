# Widgets

Widgets are domain-ready, composite enterprise components designed specifically for Northwind Traders Pvt Ltd's ERP operations. From role-based access matrixes to drag-and-drop Kanban logistics boards, widgets assemble primitives into production-ready business blocks.

## Usage guidelines

- **Business logic separation**: Widgets encapsulate UI interactions and visual states while accepting pure data props and callback handlers for business operations.
- **Data density**: Widgets support compact display modes to present high-density business intelligence without visual clutter.
- **Touch and keyboard**: All widgets include accessible touch targets and keyboard alternatives for mouse-intensive operations.
