title: Patterns
description: Reusable composition recipes and architectural patterns for enterprise ERP screens.
---

Patterns document established combinations of `@innostes/ui` primitives and widgets that resolve recurrent challenges in enterprise software systems. Rather than prescribing rigid page layouts, patterns provide battle-tested, accessible blueprints for data flows, validation, multi-device layouts, and operational workflows modeled on Northwind Traders Pvt Ltd.
