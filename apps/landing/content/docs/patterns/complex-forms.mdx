# Complex forms

Enterprise procurement, invoicing, and inventory intake require structured data collection spanning dozens of interrelated fields. The complex forms pattern orchestrates `FormLayout`, `Field`, input groups, and validation feedback into a coherent operator experience.

## Design principles

- **Sectional grouping**: Divide extensive forms into logical cards or accordion steps to reduce visual fatigue.
- **Immediate validation**: Validate fields on blur and on submit, displaying inline errors via `FieldError` linked with `aria-describedby`.
- **Dirty state protection**: Track form dirty state and warn operators before navigating away from incomplete transactions.
